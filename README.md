# 🌟 Maryam Ayra Studio - Creative Design & Digital Services Website

A modern, high-converting business and portfolio website for **Maryam Ayra Studio**, showcasing creative branding, UI/UX design, social media kits, pricing tiers, client reviews, and direct WhatsApp ordering.

## 🚀 Key Business Features
- 💼 **Professional Brand Identity**: Tailored for creative design & digital services.
- 📖 **Digital Planners & Shop**: Aesthetic GoodNotes life planners, social media kits, business finance sheets & wellness journals with instant WhatsApp ordering.
- 💬 **WhatsApp Integration**: Floating WhatsApp widget & 1-click package ordering links with prepopulated messages.
- 🎨 **Services Showcase**: Brand identity, UI/UX & web design, social media kits, print & packaging.
- 📦 **Transparent Pricing Packages**: Starter Brand, Business Pro (Most Popular), and Digital Elite tiers.
- ⭐ **Client Testimonials**: Real-style social proof with 5-star ratings and client avatars.
- 📨 **Project Inquiry Form**: Dropdown service selector with interactive client feedback.
- 🌓 **Dark & Light Mode**: Seamless theme switching with persistent local storage.
- 📱 **100% Mobile Responsive**: Looks stunning on smartphones, tablets, and desktops.

## 📁 Project Structure
```text
├── index.html   # Complete website layout, sections, and copy
├── style.css    # Modern Rose Gold & Coral glassmorphism styling
├── script.js    # Interactive features, form handling & theme switcher
└── README.md    # Documentation
```

## 🌐 Deploy to Vercel
1. Go to [vercel.com](https://vercel.com) and log in with your GitHub account.
2. Click **"Add New..."** -> **"Project"**.
3. Import **`maryam22913/my-first-website`**.
4. Click **"Deploy"** — and your business website is live to the entire world! 🎉
